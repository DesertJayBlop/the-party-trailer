Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How much does party trailer hire cost in New Zealand?

Our Basic Trailer is $349/night and includes outdoor speakers, a wireless microphone, 40 m of festoon lighting with poles, four trestle tables, 16 chairs and a full yard-games kit (cornhole, giant Jenga, Connect 4, finska, bocce). Add-ons such as marquees ($220), wedding arches ($180) or full setup & pack-down ($295) are optional.

Which cities and regions do you deliver to?

We operate from four depots: Christchurch (serving Canterbury), Queenstown/Wanaka (Southern Lakes), Auckland (North Shore to Pukekohe + Waiheke by ferry) and Wellington (Hutt Valley, Kāpiti, Wairarapa). Delivery within 50 km of each depot is included; beyond that it's $2.50/km each way.

What is the minimum hire period?

One night. We drop the trailer off the day before your event and collect it the following morning so you've got plenty of setup and recovery time.

How far in advance should I book?

Weekends over summer (Nov–Apr) are usually booked out 4–8 months ahead. Shoulder-season weekends go 6–10 weeks out. The live availability calendar above shows exactly what's open — if a date is green, grab it.

Do you set up and pack down the gear?

Self-setup is included — the trailer rolls up fully loaded with everything labelled and ready. If you'd rather be hands-off, add our Full Setup & Pack-Down service ($295) and we'll build it, test it, and come back the next morning to collect.

What deposit is required to secure a date?

A $100 refundable deposit confirms your booking. It's returned after pickup provided the gear comes back in the same condition it went out.

Can I hire for weddings and corporate events?

Yes — the wedding arch, pop-up timber bar, dance floor and photo booth are especially popular for weddings and work functions. Email hello@thepartytrailer.co.nz for a tailored quote.
